## Provenance and coverage

read, excluding Wayback scripts, wiki navigation, recent-change sidebars and
footers. The [review inventory](parity/historical/online-manual-review.json) records raw HTML
SHA-256, original/archive URLs, capture timestamps, each page's `Last-modified`,
extracted-body hashes, 56 local image references and 13 pinned source checks.
The raw archive and extracted review text remain in ignored `tmp/`; neither the
archive wrappers nor their JavaScript are application resources.

This is a collection of separately dated pages, not one uniform 2008 edition.
Most gameplay pages were last edited in January–March 2008. The footbridge copy
was last edited in 2004 and captured in 2007; most other captures are from January
2013. Troubleshooting, credits and links contain 2009/2010 edits. Page modification
time is distinct from capture time, sidebar dates and game version; a recent wiki
edit does not prove that the illustrated application changed that year.

## Changes to acceptance and help work

The [main reference's acceptance table](web-parity-manual.md#acceptance-cases-to-add-with-the-relevant-slices)
now includes MAN-A17–22 for group inheritance/reparenting, corner-based terrain
editing, new-world routes, expanded menu coverage, content-dependent recovery and
chapter-based help. These are pending fixtures, not executed tests.

P0-03 should map every `MAN-O*` requirement to source bindings and a delivered
operation, missing workflow or explicit disposition. Resolve the documented
inheritance gap and road membership without treating later documentation as an
automatic scope expansion. Preserve the original `MAN-*` IDs and Word illustration
references so prior work remains traceable.

P9-HELP should draw on both versions: use the more explicit online instructions
where they match the delivered rules, the Word illustrations where the archive
lost them, and current browser illustrations for final player instructions.
Include group-level versus per-train timetable behavior, Ctrl/Shift terrain
gestures or their portable equivalents, station no-stop diagnosis, content-set
compatibility and explicit automatic/forced appearance modes. Rebuild missing
chapter explanations from reconciled source; do not ship broken wiki links,
“not found” placeholders, Windows setup/workarounds or archive UI. Review the
expanded credits independently of the source/asset license ledger.
